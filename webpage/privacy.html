Privacy Policy

Your workouts are yours.

Asla Intervals has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your timers stay on your iPhone.

Privacy at a glance

  • 0AccountsYou never sign up or log in.
  • 0Trackers or adsNo analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • 0Servers of oursNothing is sent to us from the app.

Who we are

Asla Intervals is made by Asla in Norway. If you have a question about this policy or your data, email postmaster@asla.no.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking across apps or websites.

What's stored on your iPhone

  • Your timers – names, settings, colors and any notes you write.
  • Your appearance choice (Light, Dark or System).

This stays on your device. It's included in your iPhone backups if you use them (iCloud or a computer), according to your own settings. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Microphone and speech recognition (Voice Timer)

Voice Timer only uses the microphone after you tap the microphone button, and iOS asks for your permission first.

  • Your speech is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition. When your iPhone supports it for your language, this happens on the device. Otherwise iOS may send the audio to Apple to be processed, as described in Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • The text is turned into a timer by Apple Intelligence on your iPhone.
  • We never receive, store or listen to your audio or the text.

You can turn off access at any time in Settings → Apps → Asla Intervals.

Purchases

The Voice Timer trial and purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple gives us anonymous, combined sales reports.

When you email us

If you contact support, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We keep the conversation as long as it's needed to help you, and delete it if you ask.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see or delete any personal data we hold about you – in practice, only support emails. Contact postmaster@asla.no. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet).

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and the date below before the change is released.

Effective date: 29 October 2026
